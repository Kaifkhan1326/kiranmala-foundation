About Us

Home / About

Kiranmala Foundation

Kiranmala Foundation began in 2021 as a small group of friends feeding families in one neighbourhood. Today a network of volunteers and partners works on food, education, animals, women and the environment around Bhopal.

Every initiative runs on transparent operations and strong partnerships, so support reaches the right people.

AR

Ananya Rao

Founder & Director

What drives us

🎯

Our mission

Bring real change to people, animals and the planet through education, food, care, women's empowerment and skills.

🔍

Transparency

Clear operations and open sharing of our work, so every supporter can see where help goes.

📋

Accountability

Clear goals and regular reporting, with standards we hold ourselves to.

🤲

Collective action

We believe in the power of many hands: volunteers, partners and donors working together.

📈 Clear goals

Every project has simple targets we report on.
